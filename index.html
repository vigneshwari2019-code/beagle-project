The Beagle

The Beagle is a breed of small scent hound that is similar in appearance to the larger foxhound. The Beagle was developed primarily for hunting hare. With a great sense of smell and superior tracking instincts, the Beagle is widely used as a detection dog.

Trivia

  1. Beagles have around 220 million scent receptors.
  2. They are known for being friendly and curious.
  3. Beagles were very popular in England during the 1500s.
  4. Their long ears help trap scent particles.
  5. They are often used as detection dogs in airports.
